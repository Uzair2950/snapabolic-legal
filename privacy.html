Privacy Policy

Publisher-review draft · Updated October 27, 2026 · Not yet effective. AI scanning and live advertising are not active. A publisher-approved Android test-ad session is being prepared; its Google SDK data practices are described below. Android crash reporting remains off pending privacy review. This draft is not release approval.

1. Who is responsible

UM Ciel, based in Pakistan, publishes Snapabolic and determines how personal information is used to provide the app. Contact us for support or privacy requests at 07uzair.muhammad@gmail.com.

2. Adults only

Snapabolic is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to children. A store content rating is not permission for children to use the app. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove the affected account information.

3. Information you provide and where it goes

Account: A real email or Google account is required. Our authentication provider, Supabase, processes your account identifier, email, authentication credentials or sign-in tokens, and information needed to secure sign-in. Google processes a Google sign-in under its own terms. Signing in alone does not upload your tracking history.

Tracking on your device: Your profile, age, equation selection, height, weight, goals, activity, food and water logs, targets, recipes, body measurements and edits are stored in the app’s local database. These may reveal health-related information. Different Snapabolic accounts have separate local records. Previously signed-in users can access local tracking offline.

Optional cloud backup: Backup is a separate choice. When enabled, it sends your profile, custom foods, food/water/weight history and saved daily targets to Supabase for recovery and synchronization. Full saved recipes, body measurement records and local target-change audit records are not included in the current backup. Nutrition from a logged recipe can be included as part of your food diary. Photos are never included in this backup. Turning backup off stops further synchronization; it does not itself delete existing cloud copies.

Food lookup: Optional online food searches and product-barcode lookups send the search text or product code through our backend to the USDA food service. You can continue using the local food library when online lookup is unavailable. These lookups do not require an AI photo upload.

Purchases: RevenueCat and the relevant store process purchase, subscription, restore and entitlement information. RevenueCat receives an app account identifier and purchase/device information needed for that service. Store payment details are handled by the store; we do not ask you to enter a payment-card number into Snapabolic. The current development integration uses RevenueCat Test Store, which does not establish a paid store subscription.

Support and security: If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send. Our hosting, authentication and service providers may process IP addresses, timestamps and technical request/security logs. Do not send passwords, payment details, photos or tracking records in a support request unless there is a specific need and an agreed secure method.

4. Photos and AI processing — currently disabled

AI plate and nutrition-label analysis require a separate action and disclosure before an image is sent. When activated, the app will resize/compress selected images, remove image metadata during preparation, and send the prepared image through our authenticated backend to Google’s Gemini service. Do not include faces, identity documents or unrelated personal information. You must review and correct estimates before saving them. Barcode detection is distinct from AI image analysis.

Snapabolic does not intentionally retain raw photos in its database, cloud backup or crash reports. App-created scan cache files are removed after processing and on a later startup following interruption. This is not a promise of forensic deletion: an app or OS failure may delay cleanup, and your original gallery photo is not deleted.

Google’s treatment of API inputs depends on the configured service and region. Its unpaid-service terms may permit use of submitted content to improve products and human review. Paid-service terms differ. AI scanning will remain unavailable until the service plan, regional eligibility, retention and required disclosure/consent have been reviewed. Do not treat this draft as consent to upload images or as an assurance that Google will not retain or use them. See Google’s Gemini API terms.

AI scanning will remain unavailable in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland for now. Manual tracking, logging and account features remain available. We are preparing a server-side IPinfo country lookup for regional availability. It is off until its key, gateway checks and privacy review are complete. If enabled later, our server will send only the network IP address needed for the lookup, without GPS, account identifiers, photos or tracking records. We will use the country result to check AI eligibility; failure to establish eligibility will not authorize a scan. This integration does not install IPinfo's mobile SDK. See IPinfo's privacy policy.

5. Android crash reporting — activation pending

Firebase Crashlytics has been prepared for Android. Collection is currently disabled pending privacy review. After approval, the intended setting is on by default with a device-level control in Profile to turn it off. Reports can contain technical device/OS/app details, installation identifiers, stack traces, crash timing and fixed diagnostic codes. We do not attach your account identity, food or body records, photos, credentials or purchase records. Firebase Analytics is not enabled by this integration.

Turning sharing off stops new report collection through this integration and removes unsent reports on that installation. It does not automatically remove reports already sent to Firebase. Contact us about an access or deletion request; technical reports may not be linked to an identifiable account. Google’s handling and retention are described in Firebase’s privacy and security information. Crash-report delivery and default activation have not yet been approved or verified for release.

6. Advertising — development tests, live delivery off

Live AdMob advertising remains disabled. The publisher has approved a controlled Android development test using Google's official demonstration ads for sponsored food-library cards. Test ads do not earn revenue or scan credits. There are no ads on Home. Test requests are disabled by default outside an approved test session.

When that test is enabled, Google's Mobile Ads SDK can process the device IP address, app/device identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostics. The build blocks Android advertising-ID permissions and delays SDK measurement until initialization, but these controls do not mean that Google receives no data. Snapabolic does not attach account identity, food searches, tracking records or photos to ad requests. Requests use Google's test unit and non-personalized-ad option; this option does not replace required consent or privacy review. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Live ad units, regional consent, the AdMob GDPR message and production privacy/store forms still require review before activation. The development reward receiver is disabled; if activated after review, it will verify Google's signed callback and bind a random attempt identifier to the account that requested the ad. A client callback never creates credit. No ad callback is permission to upload a photo, and declining an ad does not prevent manual tracking.

7. Why we use information

We use information to provide account access, local tracking, explicitly enabled backup, requested food lookups and purchase verification; respond to support and privacy requests; and protect the service from misuse. Optional photo processing and crash reporting have the controls described above. We do not sell your tracking records or make your private foods, recipes or measurements public.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, core account/service processing is used to perform the service you request; optional backup and image processing depend on the relevant choice or consent; and necessary security processing may rely on legitimate interests or legal duties. Health-related information may require explicit consent or another specific basis. Required regional notices and consent must be reviewed before release in those regions; this draft does not establish worldwide compliance.

9. Retention, export and deletion

Local records remain until removed using app controls or affected by device/app storage changes. Use Profile’s CSV export for the supported tracking datasets; export is not a complete encrypted backup or an automatic export of every account/service record. Copies you save outside the app remain under your control.

Delete local data removes the signed-in account’s tracking partition from the current installation after review. It does not delete your cloud account, existing backup, other accounts or store subscription.

Delete account requests permanent removal of your active Snapabolic cloud account and personal cloud records, revokes sessions, and removes that account’s local records from the device completing the request. There is no app recovery period. An interrupted request may need to finish when connectivity returns. RevenueCat customer deletion is queued and retried; providers’ processing may not finish at the same instant as account removal. Store billing must be cancelled separately.

We cannot remotely erase exported files, screenshots, your original gallery photos or copies on offline devices. Provider backups, security logs and records that a provider must retain can outlast active-account deletion. Their production schedules and any required exceptions must be confirmed before release; no universal deletion time or unlimited-retention policy is promised in this draft. We will not keep account data merely to offer a recovery period after you have requested permanent deletion.

To request deletion outside the app, email the privacy contact from your account address. We will verify ownership without requesting your password. Do not email health or payment records.

10. Security and your choices

The app uses operating-system app storage, protected session storage, authenticated encrypted network connections and server access controls. Local database storage is not represented as independently encrypted end to end. Device backups, screen access and exported files depend on your device settings and chosen destination. Protect your device and account; no system guarantees absolute security.

You can decline optional backup and photo processing, change app permissions in Android settings, disable reminders, and, once available, control crash sharing in Profile. Revoking a permission does not itself remove records already saved. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent or make a complaint to a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise a right; we may need proportionate identity verification. Withdrawing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing.
